British Airways Review Analysis

Tool: Tableau | Dataset: British Airways Reviews + Country Mapping | Use Case: Customer Experience Insights for Aviation Sector

Project Background & Overview

British Airways collects detailed customer feedback on flight experiences including comfort, service, and aircraft type. This dashboard simulates an analyst’s role in identifying region-specific satisfaction trends and areas for operational improvement.

Built to support internal teams—such as CX leads, aircraft ops, and regional managers—this tool enables intuitive exploration of key passenger sentiment metrics.

Data Structure Overview

This project integrates two CSV datasets:

  • BA Reviews: Contains 1,000+ reviews with dimensions such as date, location, aircraft, class, traveler type, and 7+ ratings.
  • Country Mapping: Enables continent-level filtering by mapping each review location to a region.

Data was joined on place → country to simulate real-world relational modeling.

Executive Summary

  • Regional satisfaction varied significantly—Europe and Asia led in ratings, while others lagged.
  • Older aircraft models showed lower ratings across all metrics, especially in comfort and entertainment.
  • Business and solo leisure travelers were the most satisfied, with family travelers rating comfort lower.
  • A post-pandemic dip in ratings was visible across several months in 2020–2021.

Insights Deep Dive

  • Cabin Staff: Averaged >4.0 in premium classes; dipped near 3.2 on older economy aircraft.
  • Entertainment: Poor ratings tied to outdated aircraft lacking updated systems.
  • Food & Beverage: Most appreciated on Asian routes with culturally specific menus.
  • Traveler Type: Families consistently rated seat comfort below solo or business flyers.

Recommendations

  • Phase out or upgrade aircraft with persistently poor feedback.
  • Replicate successful regional menus across more international routes.
  • Tailor in-flight service strategies for economy travelers.
  • Track monthly shifts using dynamic dashboards for ongoing optimization.

Caveats & Assumptions

  • Location refers to reviewer’s input, not necessarily departure/arrival country.
  • Review content is subjective and may reflect bias or inconsistencies.
  • Lack of demographic variables limits deeper segmentation analysis.

Key Tableau Features Used

  • Interactive parameterized metric selector
  • Dynamic calculated fields and filters
  • Dual-axis visuals and custom tooltips
  • Regionally-filtered maps and summary metrics
  • Floating dashboard layout with responsive design
